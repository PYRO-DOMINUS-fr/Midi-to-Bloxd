Use different sounds for low and high notes

A sound stretched far from its natural pitch can sound thin or harsh. Low notes use the low sound, high notes use the high sound, and everything in between keeps its track's sound. Drums stay as they are.

C3
C5

Changing these replaces any sound you set on a single note. Timing, pitch, length and volume edits stay.

Why might the pitch sound off?

Bloxd shifts a sound's pitch with its rate setting. This tool can't know the exact starting pitch of every Bloxd sound, so your first test in the game may need a small nudge with Song pitch.

Edit notes

Fix anything that sounds off. Every change goes straight into your script.

Drag a note to move it, drag its right edge to change its length, double-click empty space to add a note, and press Delete to remove the selected note. The preview uses a simple synth, not Bloxd's real sounds.
